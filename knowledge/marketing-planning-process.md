# Marketing planning process

## Core statement

Marketing planning maintains awareness of the factors shaping market response and connects a business objective to analysis, strategy, implementation, and control. It should recur regularly and is especially important when introducing new products or services.[^planning]

## Process

1. Agree the business objective; when absent, develop and agree the objective before proceeding.
2. Analyse the marketing environment and organisational situation.
3. Analyse the market, identify possible segments, and make a target-market choice.
4. Set marketing objectives and strategies.
5. Formulate implementation and control metrics.
6. State the major assumptions that the plan relies on and revise them when evidence changes.[^planning]

## Conditions and limits

A plan improves discipline but does not create complete knowledge of a market, every opportunity, or all consequences of action. Objectives and plans must therefore make assumptions explicit and use control metrics to learn and adapt.[^planning]
