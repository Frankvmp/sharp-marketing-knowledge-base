# Reach, frequency, and media value

## Core statement

Media value depends on the ability to reach potential category buyers at an acceptable cost, including buyers not reached by other vehicles. Average frequency can conceal highly uneven exposure, while low cost per thousand can be less valuable than greater total reach.[^reach]

## Decision implications

Set a reach objective before selecting media. Compare total audience accumulation, duplication, exposure distribution, cost, and the incremental audience each vehicle contributes; do not optimise a single metric in isolation.[^reach]
