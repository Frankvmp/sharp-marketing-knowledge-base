# Media selection and digital risks

## Core statement

Media selection requires audience, cost, context, clutter, avoidance, and measurement considerations. Highly targeted or programmatic options can add cost and uncertainty, and digital impressions may not be viewable or human.[^selection][^digital]

## Decision implications

Accept reasonable wastage when it buys materially greater category reach. Treat engagement, clicks, targeting claims, and platform-reported metrics as inputs needing independent validation rather than proof of sales effectiveness.[^selection][^digital]

## Potential access is not actual exposure

Reach and opportunity-to-see measures describe potential access to an audience. They do not establish that a person attended to, saw, heard, or processed a particular advertisement; clutter and advertising avoidance can interrupt that path. Interpret media-delivery metrics as evidence about access, then assess advertising effects separately.[^exposure]
