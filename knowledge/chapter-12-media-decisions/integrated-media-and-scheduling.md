# Integrated media and scheduling

## Core statement

A media mix can expand reach because each additional medium may reach buyers missed by prior media. Media audiences follow size and duplication patterns, so small vehicles are not automatically uniquely valuable; sustained scheduling maintains presence between buying occasions.[^integration][^schedule]

## Decision implications

Build a mix around incremental reach, contextual variation, and cost. Use continuous or appropriately sustained schedules unless a strong category pattern justifies concentration, and evaluate the buy against the campaign objective.[^integration][^schedule]
