# Marketing executive decisions

## Core statement

Marketing executives make choices about target markets, offers, prices, communication, channels, partnerships, and resource allocation. They also represent customer and market knowledge across the organisation.[^role]

## Mechanism and relationships

Junior roles often execute defined activities, whereas senior roles make integrated decisions and work across functions, clients, and partners. Each decision has opportunity costs because spending or effort on one marketing activity constrains others.[^role]

## Evidence and data

The chapter lists decisions about products, pricing, advertising, retail partners, locations, and target consumers, and describes marketing as both a specialist function and an organisational customer voice.[^role]

## Conditions and limits

A job title does not reliably indicate decision authority. The role requires both operational knowledge and the ability to evaluate trade-offs across the organisation.[^role]

## Decision implications

An agent should define the decision, the relevant constraints, the customer evidence, and the accountable role before recommending marketing activity.
