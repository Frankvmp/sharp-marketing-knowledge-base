
# Chapter 6: Customer segmentation and targeting

This chapter distinguishes useful buyer differences from unnecessary market exclusion. Use the linked concepts to reason from evidence about market structure, buying roles, product variants, reach, and total returns.

## Concept map

- [Segmentation-based targeting](./segmentation-based-targeting.md) — decide whether a buyer difference is actionable and valuable.
- [Customer, consumer, and influencer roles](./customer-consumer-and-influencer-roles.md) — map who buys, uses, pays, and influences.
- [Brand-user profiles and market partitions](./brand-user-profiles-and-market-partitions.md) — distinguish broad brand appeal from genuine need-based divisions.
- [Broad reach, product variety, and scale](./broad-reach-product-variety-and-scale.md) — serve heterogeneous demand without excluding the wider market.
- [Targeting returns and light buyers](./targeting-returns-and-light-buyers.md) — evaluate incremental total return rather than selected-segment response.
- [Smart targeting principles](./smart-targeting-principles.md) — default to inclusive category reach unless evidence supports restriction.
