# Product features and benefits

## Core statement

Product features are attributes of an offer; benefits are the outcomes those attributes enable for customers. Product decisions should connect features to the purpose and value customers recognise.[^features]

## Design procedure

1. List the offer's observable attributes and capabilities.
2. Identify the functional outcome each attribute enables.
3. Identify the higher-order benefit that makes the outcome valuable to the customer.
4. Test whether customers can recognise and use the benefit.
5. Ensure communication makes relevant benefits salient without overstating them.[^features]

## Conditions and limits

A feature has no inherent customer value unless it helps fulfil a need in a relevant situation. Different customers may value the same feature differently, and a benefit claim must remain consistent with the total offer and actual customer experience.[^features]

## Decision implications

Use features as evidence for a customer-relevant benefit, not as the final product proposition. Prioritise attributes that improve the customer's ability to achieve the core purpose.
