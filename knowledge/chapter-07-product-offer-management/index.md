
# Chapter 7: Product offer management

This chapter treats products as total offers that fulfil a customer purpose. The concepts cover offer design, distinctive recognition, portfolio choices, innovation, lifecycle reasoning, and service delivery.

## Concept map

- [Total offer and core need](./total-offer-and-core-need.md) — How a product combines goods, services, and marketing elements to satisfy a customer purpose.
- [Product features and benefits](./product-features-and-benefits.md) — How product attributes translate into functional and higher-order customer benefits.
- [Distinctive assets and packaging](./distinctive-assets-and-packaging.md) — How names, symbols, packaging, and other distinctive assets help buyers identify offers and attribute marketing correctly.
- [Product mix, range, and B2B offers](./product-mix-range-and-b2b-offers.md) — How product ranges address heterogeneous needs and how B2B product decisions include relationship and service requirements.
- [Product innovation and adoption](./product-innovation-and-adoption.md) — How innovation type and adoption conditions shape development, market introduction, and customer uptake.
- [Product category life cycle and growth options](./product-category-life-cycle-and-growth-options.md) — How category evolution frames product, pricing, distribution, promotion, and mature-market growth choices.
- [Services, experience, and adaptation](./services-experience-and-adaptation.md) — How service delivery, experience, and changing customer circumstances shape the total offer.
