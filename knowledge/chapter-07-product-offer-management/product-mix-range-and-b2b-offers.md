# Product mix, range, and B2B offers

## Core statement

A product range offers alternative ways to fulfil a basic need across differences in price, quality, capability, and use. In B2B markets, product decisions also include the relationship, service, and capability required to create value for the customer organisation.[^range][^b2b]

## Reasoning procedure

1. Define the common need served by the range.
2. Identify genuine variation in buyer requirements, use occasions, and constraints.
3. Specify each offer's role, capability, price, channel, and target use.
4. Check whether variants expand market reach or merely add avoidable complexity.
5. In B2B, map the customer's operating process and the supplier capabilities that support it.[^range][^b2b]

## Conditions and limits

The same core item can be offered differently to industrial and consumer buyers through quantity, channel, service, and marketing mix. In organisational markets, products can include outsourced services and co-created capabilities, so the customer is often more directly involved than in consumer markets.[^range][^b2b]

## Decision implications

Manage the range as a system of offers rather than independent items. Use variety to serve material needs differences while preserving clear roles, operational feasibility, and customer value.
