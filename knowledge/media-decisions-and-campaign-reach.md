# Media decisions and campaign reach

## Core statement

Media decisions place advertising where potential category buyers can see or hear it. Media value depends on how many potential buyers it can reach, whether it reaches people other media miss, and whether it delivers an exposure likely to be noticed and remembered.[^media-summary]

## Decision procedure

1. Set a clear communication and reach objective.
2. Select media using audience reach, incremental reach, noticeability, and memorability.
3. Spread placements across time, channels, and media to expose more buyers rather than concentrating unnecessarily.
4. Use output metrics such as sales, awareness, or advertisement recall with care when evaluating impact.
5. Treat cross-media synergy and incremental reach as hypotheses when direct cross-media measurement is unavailable.[^media-summary][^cross-media]

## Conditions and limits

Separating the effect of media strategy from advertising content and other market events is difficult. Cross-media systems may not measure individual duplication, flow, or interaction adequately; claims about integrated-media effects therefore require caution.[^cross-media]
