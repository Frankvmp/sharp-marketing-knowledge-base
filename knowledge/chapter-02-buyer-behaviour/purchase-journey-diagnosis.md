# Purchase-journey diagnosis

## Core statement

A purchase journey is a diagnostic map of the stages buyers move through before and after a category purchase. It helps identify whether the constraint is awareness, consideration, access, conversion, or repurchase.[^journey]

## Procedure

1. Define the actual stages for the category and relevant buyer group.
2. Avoid copying a generic funnel without adapting its stages and sequence.
3. Measure the proportion of target buyers converting from each stage to the next.
4. Compare the brand's conversion rates with the category or relevant alternatives.
5. Identify the stage where loss is material and select an intervention matched to that constraint.[^journey]

## Conditions and limits

A new-car journey, a chocolate-bar journey, and a professional-buying journey can differ substantially. Journey stages can also differ between experienced and inexperienced buyers. A funnel maps a path; it does not prove that changing one stage caused downstream behaviour.[^journey]

## Decision use

Use the journey to diagnose the problem before selecting tactics. Do not spend on awareness when the evidence indicates that buyers already know the offer but cannot find it or do not convert at purchase.
