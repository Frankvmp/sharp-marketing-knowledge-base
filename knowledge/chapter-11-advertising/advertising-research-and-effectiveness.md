# Advertising research and effectiveness

## Core statement

Advertising research should assess whether an execution is noticed, correctly attributed, and capable of building useful brand memory. Pre-tests and biometric methods require evidence of predictive validity; sales movements alone are insufficient to judge an advertisement.[^research][^measurement]

## Decision implications

Measure correct brand linkage, quality, reach, and later brand or sales effects in context. Prefer individual-level exposure-and-purchase evidence when possible, and do not treat weekly sales reports as a direct measure of advertising effectiveness.[^research][^measurement]
