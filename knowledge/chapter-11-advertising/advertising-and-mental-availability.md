# Advertising and mental availability

## Core statement

Most advertising works through memory: it helps a brand be noticed, recognised, and thought of in relevant buying situations. Advertising must be correctly linked to the brand and reinforce useful associations, especially because many buyers purchase infrequently.[^memory][^effects]

## Decision implications

Prioritise distinctive branding and category-relevant memory structures. Treat attention, emotion, and entertainment as means to reinforce brand memory, not as the final objective.[^memory]

## Timing and interpretation of effects

Advertising effects need not appear immediately after a campaign begins, and some memory effects can persist long after advertising stops. Weekly sales are also affected by competitor activity, promotions, price changes, launches, and weather. Short-term sales movement alone is therefore not a reliable basis for deciding whether an advertisement has worked.[^timing]
