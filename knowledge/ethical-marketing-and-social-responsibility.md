# Ethical marketing and social responsibility

## Core statement

Ethical marketing decisions require consideration of both freedom of choice and responsibility to society. Responsibility is shared among businesses, consumers, government, and other stakeholders; legal compliance alone does not settle every ethical question.[^ethics]

## Decision questions

1. Who may be helped or harmed by the action?
2. What freedom of choice is preserved or constrained?
3. What responsibility does the organisation have to society, consumers, workers, and affected communities?
4. What regulation or legal boundary applies?
5. Where is self-regulation sufficient, and where is external intervention necessary?
6. What economic, social, and environmental outcomes should be assessed together?[^ethics]

## Conditions and limits

Ethical decisions are complex because stakeholders can reasonably disagree about risk, responsibility, regulation, and acceptable trade-offs. A corporate responsibility programme should be assessed against its actual social, environmental, and economic effects, not only the organisation's stated intentions.[^ethics]
