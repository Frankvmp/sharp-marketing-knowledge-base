# Market-based assets

## Core statement

Mental availability and physical availability are the two principal market-based assets. Mental availability is the likelihood that a brand is noticed, recognised, or thought of in buying situations. Physical availability is how easily or conveniently the product or service is available in a buying situation. Together, they determine how easy a brand is to buy and for how many people.[^sharp-2017-ch1-assets]

## Reasoning model

| Asset | Meaning in the chapter | Questions for an agent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mental availability | The brand is likely to be noticed, recognised, or thought of when a buyer could purchase | Will this action make the brand more likely to come to mind in relevant buying situations? |
| Physical availability | The product or service is easy or convenient to obtain in a buying situation | Will this action make the offering easier to find, access, or buy where and when buyers need it? |
| Combined effect | Both assets moderate the sales effect of marketing interventions | Does the proposal improve one asset while undermining the other, or does it support both? |

## Evidence and data

The source illustrates mental availability through recognition and recall of the Coca-Cola brand, and physical availability through widespread access in supermarkets, convenience stores, vending machines, cafés, and restaurants. It also uses Google to illustrate that intangible market-based assets can represent substantial organisational value.[^sharp-2017-ch1-assets]

## Application boundary

The chapter treats marketing interventions as contributing to the maintenance and building of these assets, not merely generating immediate sales. An agent should therefore assess a proposed action's expected effect on both current transactions and future ease of buying.[^sharp-2017-ch1-assets]

This is a decision lens, not proof that a particular intervention will succeed. The source does not supply a universal prediction for any individual campaign, price change, or distribution decision.[^sharp-2017-ch1-assets]
