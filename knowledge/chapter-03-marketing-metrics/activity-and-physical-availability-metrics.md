# Activity and physical-availability metrics

## Core statement

Marketing activity metrics record what the firm and competitors actually did. Physical-availability metrics record how easy the offer is to find and buy. Together they provide the historical context needed to interpret changes in sales and buyer behaviour.[^activity]

## Data to record

Activity records can include advertising spend, media, initiative timing, sales calls, packaging launches, price promotions, and competitor activity. Physical-availability metrics include distribution points, opening hours, geographic coverage, delivery coverage, display points, and shelf presence.[^activity][^availability]

## Reasoning rule

Collect a broad set of activity records continuously. Data that was not captured when an event occurred may be difficult or impossible to reconstruct later. A brand's presence in major chains or on a website does not establish that it is readily available in all relevant purchase situations.[^activity][^availability]

## Decision use

When sales move, examine the time series of brand and competitor actions alongside distribution and availability conditions before attributing the change to one cause.
