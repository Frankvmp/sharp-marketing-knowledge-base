# Retailing and physical availability

## Core statement

Physical availability makes an offer easy to find, access, inspect, and buy in relevant shopping situations. In retailing, location, number of outlets, parking, stock, and navigation strongly influence how many buyers a store or brand reaches.[^retailing][^location]

## Decision implications

Map the buyer's shopping journey, including where, when, and how the category is bought. Increase relevant access points and reliable stock before assuming a communication or preference problem. Store location and outlet coverage change both customer reach and the stores with which a retailer competes most directly.[^location]

## Conditions and limits

Retail formats and scale can lower prices and increase reach, but local shopping routines and established category practices constrain whether a new retailer or format will succeed.[^retailing]
