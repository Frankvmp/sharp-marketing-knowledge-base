# Online retailing and channel power

## Core statement

Online retailing expands physical availability through a different access channel. It can increase loyalty through constrained screen search and purchase-history lists, while direct-to-consumer options can partly offset retailer power without replacing retail partnerships.[^online][^channels]

## Conditions and limits

Online shopping varies by category and country; it does not automatically replace bricks-and-mortar retail. Manufacturers commonly still depend on retailers, even when direct channels or branded stores add leverage and customer access.[^online][^channels]

## Decision implications

Treat online and physical access as complementary channel choices. Evaluate how search, assortment visibility, delivery, previous-purchase lists, and retailer relationships affect buyer reach and repeat purchase.
