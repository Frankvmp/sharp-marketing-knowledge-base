# B2B selling and key accounts

## Core statement

B2B selling identifies opportunities, develops proposals, wins business, and manages continuing relationships. Key account management coordinates specialist resources around strategically important customers.[^selling][^accounts]

## Selling process

1. **Identify opportunities:** prospect for organisations likely to need the offer, then collect information about their size, needs, decision process, and potential value.
2. **Gain business:** maintain contact, investigate requirements, develop and present a proposal, negotiate terms, and coordinate the promised solution.
3. **Manage the relationship:** follow up on delivery and service commitments, resolve issues, identify further needs, and retain relationship knowledge after a lost opportunity.[^selling]

## Decision implications

Map the buying centre, prospect information needs, proposal requirements, delivery commitments, and relationship owners. Treat lost customers as future opportunities where continued relationship information remains useful.[^selling]
