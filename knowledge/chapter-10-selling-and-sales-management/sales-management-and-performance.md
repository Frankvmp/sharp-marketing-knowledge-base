# Sales management and performance

## Core statement

Sales management sets expectations, supports selling activities, evaluates performance, and provides feedback, coaching, and training. Activity-based evaluation helps diagnose where intervention is needed rather than relying only on results.[^management][^performance]

## Decision implications

Define role clarity, performance criteria, support, feedback cycles, and training needs for each selling stage. Align sales strategy and rewards with wider business and marketing strategy.[^management]
