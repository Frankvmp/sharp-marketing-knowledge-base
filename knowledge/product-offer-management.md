# Product offer management

## Core statement

Buyers respond to a total offer: the product or service, the other marketing-mix elements, market-based assets, and the brand. Product management keeps this offer relevant to existing and potential buyers; changes to price or promotion cannot reliably compensate for an offer that is unfit for the market.[^offer]

## Decision areas

- Maintain the core product's fit with buyer needs and wants.
- Manage ancillary features, including packaging and labelling.
- Identify emerging needs and opportunities for new products.
- Decide how a new product is named and introduced to the market.
- Manage the offer as its category evolves, including efforts to extend sales during category decline.
- In business markets, manage customer and supplier relationships as part of the offer.[^offer]

## Conditions and limits

The same core analysis applies to physical goods and services. Product decisions must be coordinated with the rest of the marketing mix, but product relevance is a prerequisite rather than a consequence of communication or price activity.[^offer]
