# Advertising and memory availability

## Core statement

Advertising can influence buying when it is processed and correctly associated with the advertised brand. Its central function is to refresh, and sometimes build, memory associations that make the brand easier to notice, recognise, or recall in buying situations.[^advertising]

## Mechanism

Memory associations can include what the brand does, how it looks, where it is available, when and where it is used, and who uses it. Communication that is noticed but attributed to a competitor can strengthen the wrong brand. The cited evidence reports that fewer than 20 per cent of television advertising exposures were both noticed and correctly branded, illustrating the risk of wasted exposure.[^advertising]

## Decision procedure

1. Define the buying situations and associations that need strengthening.
2. Reach a broad set of potential category buyers.
3. Ensure the advertisement captures attention and makes the brand identifiable.
4. Use clear, expected brand associations and distinctive cues.
5. Evaluate advertising research with attention to its measurement limits, rather than assuming exposure alone created effect.[^advertising-summary]
