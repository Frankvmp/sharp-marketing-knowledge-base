# Physical availability and retailing

## Core statement

Physical availability is shaped by the channels, locations, convenience, delivery, assortment, and in-store or online conditions through which buyers obtain an offer. Online and physical retail are alternative and complementary ways to reduce purchase friction, not automatically interchangeable replacements.[^retailing]

## Mechanism and relationships

Online shopping can make prior buying lists and limited screen space more prominent, but buyers already tend to use small repertoires in physical stores. Traditional retailers retain advantages where buyers value close inspection, immediate possession, or convenience. Online channels add choice, delivery, speed, and sometimes lower prices; direct-to-consumer channels can also reduce manufacturer dependence on large retailers.[^online]

## Decision implications

Assess physical availability from the buyer's perspective: whether the offer can be readily found, inspected where needed, ordered, delivered, and purchased in the relevant situation. Do not assume national presence or a website provides complete availability. Compare the convenience of each channel for the category and buyer.[^retailing]
