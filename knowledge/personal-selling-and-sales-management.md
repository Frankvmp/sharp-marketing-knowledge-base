# Personal selling and sales management

## Core statement

Personal selling is a marketing-communication activity whose role depends on the customer, the purchase complexity, and who initiates contact. It is especially important in business markets, where selling can involve strategic relationships, major accounts, and complex buying processes.[^selling]

## Decision implications

Match the sales approach to household or organisational customers, the scale and complexity of the decision, and the relationship required. Manage the sales force through clear leadership, especially where customers are large or purchasing is complex. Account for the changing effects of the internet and social media on buyer and seller activity.[^selling]

## Conditions and limits

Consumer selling is often less complex than business selling, but exceptions exist. Relationship management does not remove the requirement for ethical behaviour. Sales management must address both performance and the manner in which selling is conducted.[^selling]
