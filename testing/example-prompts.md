# Example prompts for ordinary use

Tell the agent to read the [agent-use contract](../setup/agent-use-contract.md) before answering.

- “A brand has high awareness but is not growing. What explanations should we investigate before deciding on a campaign?”
- “We surveyed a group of customers and most preferred our offer. What can and cannot be concluded from that result?”
- “How should a team distinguish a planning objective from a list of marketing tactics?”
- “What should be checked before concluding that a campaign caused a sales increase?”
- “How can a retailer diagnose whether a purchase problem is caused by memory, access, or another factor?”

These are examples only. Users may ask other questions within the release’s scope.
